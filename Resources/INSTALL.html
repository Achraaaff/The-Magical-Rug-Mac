The Magical Rug

A cloth rug for your Mac desktop. Prepared for an Intel Mac running macOS 15.7.1. The app also includes Apple Silicon support and requires macOS 14 or later.

Install once

  1. Open the downloaded disk image.
  2. Drag The Magical Rug.app onto the Applications shortcut.
  3. Open The Magical Rug from Applications.
  4. If macOS cannot verify its developer, try opening it once, then go to System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway and confirm Open. The app is ad-hoc signed, not Apple-notarized. Do not change your Mac's overall security settings.
  5. Read the short welcome screen. Leave Open automatically when I log in checked if you want the rug after a restart.

No Codex, Terminal commands, Homebrew, Qt, Xcode, or coding is needed to use the downloaded app. It does not ask for Accessibility, Screen Recording, or permission to rearrange Finder files.

Use the rug

  • Move: drag the middle.
  • Lift and fold: drag a corner toward the middle. The corner lifts over the top.
  • Lock or unlock: press Control + Option + R.
  • Resize: hold Option and drag, or choose Make Larger / Make Smaller from the rug menu.
  • Rotate: scroll over the rug, or Shift-drag.
  • Flatten: double-click the rug, or use Flatten Rug from its menu.

The rug menu is the small overlapping-rectangles icon in the menu bar. It also contains Quit, Center Rug, Open at Login, and the lock control.

When locked, the rug stays still and mouse clicks pass through to the desktop. There is no lock badge or hover toolbar on the rug. Its position, shape, size, and lock state are saved.

If something looks wrong

  • Nothing moves: press Control–Option–R once; it may be locked.
  • The shortcut is unavailable: another app may use it. Use Lock or Unlock in the rug menu.
  • The rug is on the wrong display: open the rug menu on the display you want, then choose Move to This Display. Center Rug and Flatten Rug can recover an awkward pose.
  • Automatic startup was not approved: open System Settings → General → Login Items and allow The Magical Rug, or add it manually there.
  • You want to remove it: turn off Open at Login in the rug menu, choose Quit, then move The Magical Rug.app from Applications to the Trash.

The app overlays your existing wallpaper; it does not replace it, move files, hide Finder icons, or change desktop sorting.

Validation: this version is compiled and packaged on a hosted Intel Mac. The shared interaction tests cover folds, lock, movement, state restoration, and layer crossings. The actual desktop experience has not been manually checked on a physical Mac, so a flawless first run cannot be guaranteed.
